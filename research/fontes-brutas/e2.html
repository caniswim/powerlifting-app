Garrett Blevins
Garrett Blevins the CEO of Evolve, is a world-class powerlifter and coach who earned bronze and silver medals at the IPF World Championships in 2017 and 2018, respectively. He also set a world record at the 2017 NAPF Arnold Slingshot Pro American. Garrett’s passion for strength began in high school and developed through college football before transitioning to powerlifting in 2012. His journey as an athlete taught him how to train through injuries, manage the pressures of international competition, and balance life outside of lifting. These lessons shape his approach to coaching and programming today. Combining his background in philosophy and logic with years of coaching and competition experience, Garrett created a patented AI system that provides lifters with highly personalized, flexible programming. His vision is to help others stay consistent, adapt to life’s challenges, and reach their full strength potential.
